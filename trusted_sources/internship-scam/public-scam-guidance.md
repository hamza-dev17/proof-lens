# Job Scams Guidance

The Federal Trade Commission explains that job scams often promise work while trying to obtain money or personal information from job seekers.

Guidance patterns relevant to ProofLens include upfront fees, requests for bank or identity details before a real interview or hiring process, fake checks, pressure to move quickly, and recruiter messages from personal email or messaging accounts.

This source supports internship and job-post risk analysis. It is not university evidence and must not be used as an Official University Source.
